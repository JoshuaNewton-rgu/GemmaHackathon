Open weights = the ethics

Screen-watching through a closed cloud API is surveillance. On your own machine, it's a study partner. The privacy property isn't a feature of our code — it's a property of Gemma being open.

Right-sized intelligence

One family, two jobs: a small fast model where privacy and latency live, a bigger one where reasoning lives. That's the Gemma 4 size ladder used as an architecture, not a menu.

Semantic, not syntactic

A window title says "YouTube". Gemma 4's vision says "a thermodynamics lecture on YouTube, which this student's contract allows". No blocklist can do that.

Honest by design

Screen perception is becoming a commodity — the product is the behavioural loop: contract → semantic verdict → negotiation → autopsy → adapted plan. Gemma makes the loop private enough that people will actually turn it on.

The phone answer

"What if I procrastinate on my phone?" Three layers: the camera sees the glance, idle inference sees the absence, and the work diff sees the truth — 20 phone-minutes is an empty diff. You can hide a phone from a camera. You can't hide an empty page.

Laptop → pocket (roadmap)

The same open family runs on-device on Android (Gemma 4 E2B via AI Edge). A phone companion isn't a rewrite — it's the same weights in your pocket, still private. One model family, every device you procrastinate on.
